# HMN Agri Trade ERP — Phases 11–13

## Phase 11: Professional Expenses

Fuel, Salary, Transport, Warehouse, Electricity, Office, Repair and Miscellaneous categories; category management; document attachments; payment methods; date/category/status filters; approval/rejection; monthly totals and detailed expense reporting.

## Phase 12: Advanced Reports

Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly periods; Profit & Loss; cash flow; warehouse stock; customer ledger; supplier ledger; inventory valuation; expense category report; tax report; village-wise sales; product-wise profit; PDF, Excel-compatible XLS and CSV exports.

## Phase 13: Professional Documents

Branded invoice PDF with company information, QR, Code-128 barcode, signature area, terms, print/download, SMTP email attachment and WhatsApp sharing; quotation PDF; delivery challan PDF; and payment receipt PDF.
