# Phase 15 AI Business Assistant audit

Implemented:

- AI dashboard with daily, weekly and monthly summaries
- Natural-language business queries for pending balances, profit, stock, customers, sales, villages, outstanding cash, fast/slow movers and forecasts
- Sales, purchase, expense and profit forecasting
- Inventory demand and reorder forecasting
- Seasonal demand ranking
- Price-margin review suggestions
- Customer credit-risk warnings
- Slow-moving and fast-moving product insights
- Cross-module smart search for customer, product, invoice, village, supplier, vehicle, driver, barcode and QR
- Automatic report summaries, warnings and recommended actions
- AI query history and audit logging
- Role-based ai.view and ai.query permissions
- Optional OpenAI-compatible provider adapter; deterministic local ERP analytics remains the fallback

Forecasts are decision-support estimates based on available historical data and are not guarantees.
